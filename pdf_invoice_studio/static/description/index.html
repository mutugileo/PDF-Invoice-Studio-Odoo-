What it adds to Odoo

Status watermark

A diagonal PAID, PARTIALLY PAID, OVERDUE, DRAFT, CANCELLED or REVERSED stamp, driven by the invoice's own status. Odoo has no watermark of any kind, so today that status is only readable from the payment table at the foot of the page. Pick the colour, the strength, and which statuses are stamped. The stamp prints as a hollow outline so the amounts underneath stay legible.

Dynamic address collapsing

When the shipping address simply repeats the billing address, Odoo prints it twice. This hides the duplicate and widens the remaining block so there is no empty gap. Choose always show, hide when it matches, or never show — the last is useful for digital and service businesses with nothing to ship.

Company seal and authorised signature

Upload an official seal and a signature image, with the signatory's name and job title printed beneath. Expected on invoices in many jurisdictions, and not a field Odoo has.

Your own fiscal label

Odoo prints a hardcoded “Tax ID”. Print KRA PIN, GSTIN, VAT No or whatever your tax authority calls it, on both your company and your customer. Add your company registration number to the header, and set the document heading to TAX INVOICE or your own wording.

Payment instructions block

A formatted panel below the totals for bank details, IBAN/SWIFT, mobile money paybills or any transfer instructions, plus a footer note for your payment terms.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module from Apps.
  2. Go to Invoicing → Configuration → Settings.
  3. Open the PDF Invoice Studio section and fill in your details.
  4. Print any invoice.

Every setting lives on the company record, so multi-company databases keep separate branding per company. Nothing is written to the invoice itself, so changing a setting takes effect on the next print.

Worth knowing before you buy

  • The status stamp prints on the first page of an invoice, the way a physical stamp is applied. It does not repeat on continuation pages.
  • Built and tested against Odoo 17.0. It has not been verified on 16.0 or 18.0.
  • This extends Odoo's standard invoice report through clean template inheritance. It does not replace the report, so other modules that extend the same report continue to work.
  • Odoo 17 already includes a bulk “Export ZIP” action. This module's export differs in two ways: it always renders the final invoice rather than falling back to a pro-forma for invoices that have not been printed yet, and it includes vendor bills.
